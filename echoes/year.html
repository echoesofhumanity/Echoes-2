🌟 Echo of the Year

One Story.
One Year.
One Echo.

Remember.
Honor.
Inspire.

Every year, countless acts of kindness, courage and compassion happen around the world.

Echo of the Year brings one of those stories forward and gives it a place in our collective memory.

A Year of Humanity

A year contains millions of human moments. Some are difficult. Some are painful. Some remind us of what humanity must change.

But among them are also moments of extraordinary kindness, courage, sacrifice, solidarity and hope.

People helping people. Communities standing together. Strangers protecting strangers. Individuals choosing compassion when they could have chosen indifference.

Echo of the Year exists to make one of those moments visible.

Not because it is the only story that matters. But because one story can sometimes remind millions of people of what is possible.

More Than a Recognition

Echo of the Year is about remembering what we want humanity to become.

❤️ Compassion

A moment when someone chose to care about another person's life, dignity or suffering.

🛡️ Courage

A person who acted when fear, difficulty or uncertainty could have stopped them.

🤝 Solidarity

People standing together and refusing to leave others behind.

🌱 Hope

A story that shows that even after hardship, people can rebuild and begin again.

How We Choose

The story comes first. Always.

Echo of the Year is not a popularity contest and it is not a competition for fame.

We look beyond visibility, status and influence. What matters is the human action behind the story and the difference that action made.

Throughout the year, the stories recognized as Echoes of the Month become part of a wider conversation about humanity.

At the end of the year, those stories are revisited alongside new stories shared with us from around the world.

From this collection, one story is selected to become the Echo of the Year.

What We Look For

The qualities that make a human story worth carrying forward.

Compassion

A genuine act of care toward another person, community or living being.

Courage

Choosing to act despite fear, difficulty, uncertainty or personal risk.

Solidarity

Standing beside people who need support and refusing to leave them behind.

Dignity

Protecting the dignity, rights and humanity of another person.

Positive Impact

Creating meaningful change in a person's life, a community or the wider world.

Inspiration

A story that encourages others to believe that they too can make a difference.

Humanity Has No Borders

The Echo of the Year can come from anywhere.

It may come from a crowded city or a remote village.

It may come from a humanitarian worker, a teacher, a child, a neighbor, a doctor, a volunteer, a community or a complete stranger.

The story may be known by millions of people, or it may have happened quietly between two people who never expected recognition.

Geography does not determine the value of humanity.

Neither does fame.

The Echo of the Year

One story carrying the human spirit of a whole year.

2026 Echo of the Year

This is where the story of the year's selected Echo will live.

The complete story will be told here: who they are, what happened, who they helped, what they changed and why their story deserves to be remembered.

We will not reduce a person's experience to a headline or a single photograph.

The story comes first. The recognition follows.

Because the purpose of Echo of the Year is not to create another celebrity.

It is to make humanity visible.

Why It Matters

Recognition can become a form of remembrance.

See

We make positive human action visible in a world often dominated by negative headlines.

Remember

We preserve meaningful human moments so they do not disappear with the passing of time.

Honor

We acknowledge people and communities whose actions reflect the values we believe humanity needs.

Inspire

We hope one person's action gives another person the courage to act.

The Humanity Archive

A growing record of the people and stories that remind us what humanity can be.

Every Echo of the Year becomes part of a permanent collection of human stories.

Over time, these stories will form a living archive of compassion, courage, solidarity and hope from around the world.

A future generation should be able to look back and find more than a record of conflict, suffering and division.

They should also find evidence that people helped one another.

That people protected strangers.

That communities stood together.

That kindness existed.

And that humanity never stopped trying.
